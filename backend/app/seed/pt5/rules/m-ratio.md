# Ratios and conditional probability
**Rule.** "3 to 5" compares two parts. The whole has 3 + 5 = 8 parts, so the first part is 3/8 of the total.
**Your trap.** You used 3/5 of the total, or you divided by the wrong total in a two-way table.
**Method.**
1. Ratio: add the parts, find one part (total ÷ parts), then multiply.
2. Two-way table: find the group after "if", "given", "of those", or "from those who". Its total is the denominator.
3. Inside that row or column, find the cell you want. That is the numerator.
**Watch for.**
- "15 more girls than boys" is the difference of parts (7 - 4 = 3 parts), not one part.
- "Given positive, probability of no condition" is not the same as "given no condition, probability of positive".
- Never divide by the grand total when the question names a group.
