# Scale factor: area and volume
**Rule.** If lengths are in the ratio a : b, areas are in the ratio a² : b² and volumes in the ratio a³ : b³. A quantity that is proportional to area (or volume) follows the same ratio.
**Your trap.** Sides 3 : 1 give areas 9 : 1. You multiplied the total flux by 9. The larger square gets 9/10 of the total, not 9 times the total.
**Method.**
1. Write the length ratio.
2. Square it (area) or cube it (volume).
3. Add the parts: 9 + 1 = 10 parts.
4. One part = total / number of parts. Multiply by the parts you need.
**Watch for.**
- Your answer for one piece must be smaller than the total.
- Do not use the length ratio itself for an area or a mass.
- Read which piece the question asks for: the larger or the smaller.
- If the question gives the difference, use the difference of the parts: 9 - 1 = 8.
