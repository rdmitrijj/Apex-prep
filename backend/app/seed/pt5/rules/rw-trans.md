# Transitions
**Rule.** The transition names how sentence 2 relates to sentence 1.
**Your trap.** You picked "However" for a result ("so people wrongly assume ..."), "For example" for a consequence, "Regardless" for a specific detail, and "For instance" for an opposite case.
**Method.**
1. Cover the choices. Read the sentence before and the sentence after.
2. Name the relationship in one word from the list below.
3. Pick the only choice with that meaning.
**Watch for.**
- Result: Hence, Thus, Accordingly, Consequently, Therefore. "Wrongly assumed" can be a result.
- Detail of the same point: Specifically, In particular. If sentence 1 names only one change, "For example" is wrong.
- Example (one case of many): For instance, For example.
- Opposite case: Conversely, In contrast. Concession or limit: That said, Granted, Still, Nevertheless.
- Replacement: Instead. Addition: Moreover. Similarity: Likewise.
- Emphasis: In fact. Restatement: In other words. Purpose: To this end.
- Method, then its result: In so doing. Time: Later, Meanwhile.
- "Regardless": sentence 2 happens no matter what sentence 1 says.
