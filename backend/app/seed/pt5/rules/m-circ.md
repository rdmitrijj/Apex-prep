# Circles and right central angles
**Rule.** The radius is the distance from the center to any point on the circle: r² = (horizontal step)² + (vertical step)². If angle ACB at the center C is a right angle, then AB = r√2.
**Your trap.** You doubled one distance and got 2√102. The point was (h + 1, k + √102), so r² = 1 + 102 = 103 and AB = √206.
**Method.**
1. Write the horizontal and vertical steps from the center to the point.
2. Square both steps and add them. This is r². Ignore h and k.
3. Right angle at the center: AB² = r² + r² = 2r².
4. Not in center form? Complete the square: add (half the coefficient)² to both sides.
**Watch for.**
- Square the small step too: (h + 2, k + √45) gives 4 + 45.
- The right side of (x - h)² + (y - k)² = 36 is r², so r = 6.
- With fractions, use one common denominator before you add.
