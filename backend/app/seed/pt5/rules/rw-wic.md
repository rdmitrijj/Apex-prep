# Words in Context
**Rule.** The right word fits the logic of the whole sentence, not just its topic.
**Your trap.** You chose by first impression: "redefined" for engineers who *used* a known effect ("exploited"), "rationalize" where a fix was needed ("ameliorate"), and you missed the double negative in "undermined claims that his works were ___" ("inconsequential").
**Method.**
1. Cover the choices. Find the clue: a colon, an example, a contrast word, or what happens next.
2. Predict a simple word of your own ("used", "improve", "unimportant").
3. Pick the choice that means your word.
**Watch for.**
- Secondary meanings: *exploit* = make good use of; *sanction* = approve (or punish); *check* = hold back.
- Negation flips the blank: "far from ___", "refused to ___", "undermined claims that X was ___". Predict the opposite of what the evidence shows.
- All four choices may fit the topic ("rapid" snowmelt). Only one fits the clue ("patchy": bare slopes next to snowy ones).
- Mixed signals (complaints *and* pride) need a word that covers both sides ("ambivalence").
