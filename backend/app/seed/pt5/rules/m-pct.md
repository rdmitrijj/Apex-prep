# Percentages
**Rule.** Increase BY p%: multiply by (1 + p/100). Increase TO p% (or "p% of"): multiply by p/100.
**Your trap.** "Increasing x by 400% gives 60." You used 4x = 60. The right equation is x + 4x = 5x = 60, so x = 12.
**Method.**
1. Turn every change into a factor: +25% is 1.25, -20% is 0.8, "by 150%" is 2.5.
2. Successive changes: multiply the factors. Never add or subtract the percents.
3. Reverse percent: if you know the result, divide by the total factor.
**Watch for.**
- "300% greater than b" is 4b; "300% of b" is 3b.
- +20% then -20% is 96%, not 100%.
- A discount of 40% leaves 60%: multiply by 0.6.
