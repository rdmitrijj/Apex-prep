# Horizontal asymptotes
**Rule.** Compare the highest powers of x on top and on the bottom.
**Your trap.** You used the term written first. In (7 - 3x²)/(4x² + 5x), the leading term on top is -3x², not 7.
**Method.**
1. Find the highest power in the numerator and in the denominator, wherever it is written.
2. Top degree lower: the asymptote is y = 0.
3. Degrees equal: y = (leading coefficient on top) / (leading coefficient on the bottom).
4. Keep the signs: -3x² has coefficient -3.
**Watch for.**
- In a product like (2x - 1)(3 - x), multiply the x-terms: 2x · (-x) = -2x².
- The constant ratio (like 7/5) is the y-intercept, not the asymptote.
- *Desmos check:* graph it and zoom out; the tails level off at the asymptote.
